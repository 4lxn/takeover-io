Privacy

takeover.io · last updated October 29, 2026

takeover.io is a browser game. We store only what we need to save your progress and run multiplayer matches. We don't sell your data and we don't show you targeted ads.

What we store

DataWhyKept for
An account id (a random identifier), created automatically the first time you playTo save your progress without asking you to sign upUntil you delete the account; guest accounts idle for 90 days with under 100 XP are removed
Your email, or your Apple or Google account identifier, if you choose to sign in with oneSo you can sign in and keep your progress on other devicesUntil you delete the account
Your display nameShown in matches and on leaderboardsUntil you change it or delete the account
Progress and stats: lifetime XP, level, skins, games, round wins, deletions, best roundThe game's progression and your profileUntil you delete the account
Match history: mode, XP, placement and time of each matchYour recent matches and the weekly leaderboardUntil you delete the account

While you play online, the game server sees your connection and your in-game actions in order to run the match. It does not keep them after the match.

Settings such as sound, controls and difficulty are stored only in your browser.

Who processes it

Accounts and progress are stored with Supabase, and the game runs on Render. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they confirm your identity to us; we receive only what's needed to sign you in.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards show your display name and XP to other players. Nothing else about you is public.

Deleting your data

Open the game, then go to Account → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your account, profile, progress and match history. You disappear from the leaderboards right away.

Children

takeover.io is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions about your data: open an issue on GitHub.
