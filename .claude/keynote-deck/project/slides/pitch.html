The pitch

Rogue AIs, one arena

A browser .io game. Each player is a rogue AI loose in a dark datacenter, and only one gets to take it over.

Collect data

Grab glowing data packets for XP and level up your model.

Delete rivals

Firing is automatic. Out-move, out-upgrade and out-item the other AIs.

Take over

Lead when the 5-minute round ends and the datacenter is yours.

The whole game in three verbs: collect, delete, take over. Rounds are five minutes, so a match is short and anyone can drop in.
