One round

Five minutes, four beats

01

Spawn

Drop into the datacenter and start grabbing data.

02

Level up

Pick 1 of 3 upgrades. Every 3rd level: a rare augment.

03

Fight

Auto-fire, dash through shots, throw items.

04

Take over

Top AI at 5:00 controls the datacenter. Next round in 10 s.

Walk the loop left to right. Dashing through shots at the right moment is a perfect dodge that resets the dash. Upgrades come every level; augments are rarer, every third level. When the clock hits zero the leader takes over and a new round starts ten seconds later.
